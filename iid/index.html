What the proof establishes

The supplement justifies pruning through bounds valid for every original constraint in a rate subtree. It also proves that the table adjustments, numerical interpolation, and arithmetic error allowances preserve feasibility. The threshold and all rate and state grids are fixed rational inputs.

The numerical evaluator, exact inputs, and checkpoints are unchanged. The proof is the primary implementation justification. The current manifests cover the guarded launchers, documentation, and historical records as distributed.

Reproduce locally

Use Python 3.9 or later and a GNU C++17 compiler with OpenMP and signed 128-bit integers. From the complete release directory:

python3 reproduce.py --check-only
python3 reproduce.py --full --threads 8

Download the complete repository to preserve the directory structure. Check-only validates the inputs; full mode also reconstructs every layer and compares the exact objective and final checkpoint hashes. Allow tens of minutes and approximately 1.5 GB for full-run outputs.
